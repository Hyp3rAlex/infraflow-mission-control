Priority score

score = 100 x (0.35 x impact + 0.25 x urgency + 0.25 x risk reduction + 0.15 x strategic alignment) / 5

CriterionScaleWeight
Business impact0-535%
Urgency0-525%
Risk reduction0-525%
Strategic alignment0-515%

Bands: 80 and above Highest, 65 High, 45 Medium, 25 Low, below 25 Lowest. The band is stored as the Jira priority.

The score decides the order of review. It never approves a demand. Approval is a recorded decision by an authorised person after the gates have been checked.

Decision gates

GatePasses whenIf it fails
InformationAll mandatory fields are filled and no question to the requester is openReturn to requester
FundingCost-centre forecast + cost of the demand inside the budget period does not exceed the period budgetDefer, reduce scope, or reallocate budget
CapacityEffort fits into the unallocated hours of the delivery team between today and the required-by dateDefer, re-plan, or release other work
Security reviewIf the demand is security-sensitive, the Information Security Office has passed the reviewNo approval possible; this gate cannot be overridden

The gates look at one demand at a time. Before a board meeting the analyst runs the what-if comparison for the whole agenda, because hours and budget can only be used once.

Decision authority

DecisionAuthorised role
Reject at triage or route outDemand manager
Approve, defer or reject up to 50,000 EUR one-time costInfrastructure Demand Board Chair
Approve above 50,000 EUR one-time costHead of Digital Infrastructure
Approve against a failed funding or capacity gateSame approver, with a written exception rationale in the decision record

Every decision record contains: approver role, date, outcome, rationale, the finance and capacity snapshot it was based on, a follow-up owner and a follow-up date. A deferral always has a next review date.

Options

For demands above 20,000 EUR or 150 hours the solution architect documents at least two options with one-time cost, monthly cost, 12-month total, effort, benefit, delivery risk and assumptions.
