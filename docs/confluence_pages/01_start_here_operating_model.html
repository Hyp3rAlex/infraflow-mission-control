All content in this space is synthetic. Orion Systems Engineering is a fictional organisation used for a portfolio demonstrator. No real company data is stored here.

Purpose

Infrastructure Demand Management is the single entry point for requests to build or change IT infrastructure at the Munich, Toulouse, Madrid and Bremen sites. It makes sure that every request is understood, costed, checked against budget and team capacity, decided by an accountable person and handed to delivery with clear conditions.
