Limits of the forecast

  • It is a commitment-based scenario, not a statistical prediction: four months of data do not support a trend model.
  • It assumes that open purchase orders are invoiced in full inside the period.
  • It contains only approved work. Requests that are not decided are shown separately as scenarios.
  • Totals are in EUR. A non-EUR invoice is excluded and reported in its own currency until Finance approves a conversion rate; it is never added to a EUR figure.
