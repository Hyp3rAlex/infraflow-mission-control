All content in this space is synthetic. Orion Systems Engineering is a fictional organisation used for a portfolio demonstrator. No real company data is stored here.

When it is used

After an approval and before any delivery work starts. The demand moves to stage-handoff when the demand manager issues the checklist and to stage-in-delivery only when the delivery lead has accepted it. Delivery without an accepted handoff is reported as an exception.

Copy for a new handoff

  • Stable references: Demand id, Jira key, decision record
  • Approved scope: Option approved, sites and capacity in scope, what is explicitly out of scope
  • Cost envelope: Approved one-time and monthly amounts, cost centre, purchase order number
  • Target window: Start month, required-by date, maintenance windows, hours allocated per month
  • Prerequisites: Security review result, contracts, dependencies on other demands
  • Acceptance conditions: How the requester confirms the result; operational readiness (monitoring, backup, documentation)
  • Risks carried into delivery: Open risks with owner
  • Sign-off: Demand manager (complete), delivery lead (accepted)
