All content in this space is synthetic. Orion Systems Engineering is a fictional organisation used for a portfolio demonstrator. No real company data is stored here.

Purpose

How budget, actual spend, commitments and forecast are consolidated every week and at month end, and how invalid data is treated.

Procedure

  1. Export budgets, invoices and purchase orders as of the cutoff date.
  2. Run python -m infraflow validate. Every rejected row appears in the exception register with a reason, an owner and a due date.
  3. Reconcile each open purchase order: recognised amount = sum of its valid invoices.
  4. Compute actuals, open commitments and the forecast per cost centre.
  5. If a critical exception is open, publish the report as provisional with the banner and the excluded amounts.
  6. Finance controller resolves the exceptions in the source ledger; nothing is corrected in the report.

No double counting

When an invoice arrives for a purchase order, the amount moves from commitment to actual. Only the part that is not yet invoiced remains a commitment: outstanding = max(approved commitment - recognised actual, 0), for open purchase orders only. Closed and cancelled orders carry no commitment.
